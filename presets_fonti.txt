Block presets of the Real plugins: sources (2026-10-19)
Conversion of clock hours to a 0-10 knob: 7:00 = 0, 12:00 = 5, 17:00 = 10 (one hour = one unit; "Max" = 10).
Only settings with a citable source. Not created because there is no reliable source: Rangemaster, DS-1, Tone Bender MkII, Phase 90, Dyna Comp, Hendrix/Fuzz Face (no documented value beyond the manual), CE-2, CE-1, AD9, Micro Flanger, Small Stone, Wah.
